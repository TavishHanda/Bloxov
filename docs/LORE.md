# Bloxov: No Man's Land

The game's world and story. **The owner writes the lore**; this file keeps it organized. Anything marked
**TBD** is waiting on the owner: don't fill it in with invented names or story. Maps, items and characters should
tie back to what's written here.

## Tone (owner, 2026-10-10)
- A **military** setting, but **not strictly realistic**. Characters, gear, uniforms and factions can be stylized
  and game-like: the owner wants freedom to design characters, so don't hold designs to real-world military
  accuracy. It should still read as soldiers at war, not fantasy (see `docs/ART_SPEC.md` §5).

## The world
- Two nations, **Novarra** and **Exion**, were the main powers ruling the world (smaller nations elsewhere may be
  added later). Now they're at war, and it has gone **global** (names: owner, 2026-10-10).
  - **Novarra**: what it's like **TBD**. Its army is **NOVA** (the light side).
  - **Exion** ("X-one" said fast; owner, 2026-10-10): what it's like **TBD**. Its army is **EXION** (the dark
    side), and the **X1** mark shows up on EXION gear (later: uniforms and skins).
    - **The Exion Brotherhood** (named after the owner's friend group, the X1 Brotherhood): a powerful group inside
      Exion that controls it, the order behind its government and army. Details **TBD** (owner).
  - NOVA and EXION are short names for patches and voice chat, with a light vs dark look.
  - **Players don't pick a side** (owner, 2026-10-10: picking would get in the way of characters and skins). Players are
    **REVENANT** contractors instead (see below), who take contracts from either side.
  - What started the war: see the timeline below.
- The world is **mostly abandoned**, with a few **safe havens** left. TBD: what they are, who runs them, and
  whether the hideout is one.
- Who the player is, and why they go on raids: a **REVENANT** contractor (see "REVENANT" below).
- Scavs and Raiders: who they are in this world: **TBD**
- **Bon** (owner, 2026-10-11): the boss of **Bloxov Frontlines**. In the game he holds the town hall and the
  bunker under it with his guards, in heavy armor and a Combat Helmet, and carries the Bunker Key, an **Exion
  Medal** (a Brotherhood medal with the X1 mark, "only their best get one") and his own dog tag. Who he is and how he

## Timeline (owner's draft, 2026-10-10; can change)
1. **The war starts.** Something sparks war between Novarra and Exion. What exactly: **TBD**.
2. **The super-soldier program.** Novarra finds out the Exion Brotherhood is secretly making **super soldiers**,
   and strikes the program.
3. **The attack goes wrong.** The strike breaks the program open: super soldiers and **failed experiments** get
   loose in the open. Huge regions are wasted.
4. **The game: years later.** Most of the world is abandoned and the war still goes on. Some regions are still
   valuable, so **PMCs** (the players, REVENANT contractors) are sent in to gather resources. Sending whole armies
   costs too much, so Novarra, Exion and the safe havens hire contractors instead.
   - What they gather: **TBD**. One option: the Brotherhood's research (and whatever made the super soldiers)
     plus pre-war stockpiles left in those zones.
- **Distance from ground zero sets the region:** Bloxov is far from it, so the intro region stays grounded and
  normal. Regions closer to the disaster get stranger and harder.

## REVENANT: the players (owner's draft, 2026-10-10; can change)
- **REVENANT** ("one who returns") is an **independent contractor network**. On the surface it works for whoever
  pays: Novarra, Exion and the safe havens all hire it to pull resources out of the wasted zones, for money and gear.
- **Secretly revolutionary:** it's made of people both nations failed, and it works to end both powers' grip on the
  world. A bigger story to uncover later.
- **Who joins:** deserters from both armies, safe-haven survivors, even **escaped experiments**. That's why any
  character, look or species fits (see Tone).
- **PvP:** two contractors in one raid can be on rival contracts, so fighting each other makes sense.
- **A typical member's story** (loose, so any character still fits):
  1. Grows up in a border town like Bloxov, caught between Novarra and Exion, living under each side's rule at
     different times.
  2. The failed strike wastes their region. They lose home or family and end up in a safe haven, scavenging.
  3. Years in the dead zones make them tough; ex-soldiers drifting through the haven teach them to fight.
  4. REVENANT notices them and recruits them. That's where the player starts, as a Recruit.
- **How it maps onto the game:**
  - **Ranks = progression:** Recruit, Operator, Veteran, Elite (names **TBD**, owner), earned by surviving raids
    and leveling up; higher ranks unlock better contracts, gear and maybe cosmetic badges.
  - **Traders = brokers/handlers**, each working for a different client (e.g. a Novarra officer, an Exion
    Brotherhood agent, a safe-haven quartermaster). Their **contracts are the quests**.
  - **Hideout = a REVENANT outpost** where contractors rest, gear up and train between raids.

## The escape: why a REVENANT does all this (owner, 2026-10-13; planned, not in the game yet)
- Every raid, every dollar, every trader favour and every quest is for one thing: **getting out of the war**. The
  goal is to **craft an escape vehicle** and reach a **safe haven** (maybe a bunker) where the war can't follow.
- The haven doesn't take just anyone: getting in **costs a lot of money**, in the story and in the game.
- The **traders** are connected to the way out: their favours, contacts and contracts (quests) are how you get the
  parts, the route and the way in. A long quest line leads there.
- It's tied to the **characters**: when you extract with a character in the escape vehicle, that character earns a
  **skill point** to **prestige**, spent on upgrading its traits (see `docs/ROADMAP.md` "Character prestige").

  2. What the safe haven is: a bunker, a city, an island? Who runs it, and is it one of the "few safe havens left"
     above? Is the hideout one?
  3. Which traders help, and how (one per part? one knows the route? one gets you in?).
  4. After a character escapes: does it come back to fight again (the prestige loop), does the player keep their
     stash, and is the escape something you do once per character or again and again?
  5. Solo, co-op or both: does your squad escape together?
  6. How it fits REVENANT's secret goal (ending both powers' grip): is the haven part of that, or does escaping
