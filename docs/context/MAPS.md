# Maps, collision, navigation and the far view

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-13. Read before changing a map, a building or outdoor model, anything that
collides, or the AI's navigation. Art sizes and pipeline: `docs/ART_SPEC.md`. Layout plan: `docs/MAP_PLAN.md`.

- Collision layers: 1 world, 2 player, 3 enemies (bit value 4), 4 interactables (bit value 8), 5 doors (bit value 16,
  world-art branch), 6 tree tops (bit value 32: only the AI's sight and shots check it, `BoxMap.LEAVES_LAYER`), 7 bushes (bit
  value 64: only the AI's sight, `BoxMap.BUSH_LAYER`), 8 barred windows (bit value 128: stops players and vaulting,
  not bullets, `BoxMap.BARS_LAYER`), 9 glazed windows (bit value 256: stops people until the pane is broken, never
  shots, `BoxMap.WINDOW_LAYER`; the panes still whole come from each building's `<name>_panes.json`, drawn and broken
  by the map, and breaks are sent online by pane number), 10 props (bit value 512: furniture and the like, and windows with something across them since 0.23.3 (gen `shut_windows`; the motel's stay open, their blinds swing aside: `scripts/blinds.gd`; plain windows open again since 0.25.35, decided by `tools/window_audit.gd` -> `art_source/buildings/window_audit.json`: re-run it after rebuilding a building model, then the generator), stops players and vaulting only, never the AI,
  shots or eyes, not in the nav bake, `BoxMap.PROPS_LAYER`, map flag 5 -> `prop_boxes`, 0.22.0).
- Outdoor pieces that aren't box-shaped (rocks, banks, crater rims, rubble, the train) collide with triangles from
  their models (0.18.3): after changing them run the generator, `art_source/scripts/make_outdoor.py`, then the
  generator again. `tools/collision_view.gd` draws what collides over the models.
- Maps: the real map is `scenes/maps/old_bloxov.tscn`, generated by `tools/gen_old_bloxov.py` (edit the script and
  re-run it, never the scene); `RaidMap` swaps it into `main.tscn`, whose built-in test map the smoke test uses.
  The ground has hills (0.11.17): build new pieces at height 0 with `box`/`obox` and `terrain()` lifts them onto it
  (composite props inside `with group():`); see `docs/MAP_PLAN.md` "Ground".
  After changing the map's geometry, re-bake the enemy navigation: `godot --headless -s tools/nav_bake.gd` (0.29.3;
  the smoke test checks the bake matches, otherwise every raid bakes it at the start, ~1.3 s).
- Far view (0.17 world art, outdoor step 10): buildings switch to a baked far mesh 70 m out (`scripts/far_view.gd`,
  `assets/models/far/`), trees to cheap far cards 110 m out (`scripts/trees.gd`). After rebuilding a building model,
  re-bake it: `xvfb-run -a godot --rendering-driver opengl3 -s tools/far_bake.gd -- <model>` (the smoke test checks one exists).
- Enemy navigation: `scripts/nav_baker.gd` bakes the walkable area at raid start from nodes in the `nav_source`
  group (CSG boxes + world-layer box colliders). New solid map pieces must be under `Level`/`Loot` (or in that group).
- Ladders (0.35.27): the origin is the foot, it leans on what's at -Z (`into()`), the climber is at +Z. After adding or moving one, run `godot --headless -s tools/ladder_audit.gd` (the floor it leads to must be on the side it leans on; the barn's was backwards until 0.35.27).
- Riptide's models (art pass from 0.35.31; plan: the "Riptide art plan" doc): `tools/gen_riptide.py` wraps boxes in
  `with modeled(name, x, z)` as gen_old_bloxov does; `python3 art_source/scripts/make_riptide.py <name>` draws them on
  Riptide's own texture sheet (`assets/textures/riptide_atlas.png`, material `riptide_atlas.tres`, same shader as the kit
  atlas), so Frontlines never changes; then re-run the generator and import. Kit textures are painted once into
  `art_source/textures/riptide/` (delete one to repaint it). The gray box stays the collision: draw nothing a person
  can walk into without a box behind it. `tools/riptide_view.gd` has `ship-*` and `sign-*` views (`view=a,b nospawn`),
  `tools/perf_view.gd -- map=riptide web` measures draw calls. Models a person can stand in far from their middle
  (the 214 m hull) are in `FarView.NO_FAR` instead of getting a far mesh.
- The bridge tower (0.36.18): `bridge_tower` (origin 48, 0, 90) is drawn by `make_riptide.py bridge_tower`
  (`build_tower`). Its furniture is `TOWER_PROPS` in the generator (boxes with a `kind`, drawn as the real thing);
  the generator's `wall()` writes each modeled wall's doors and windows to the model's json (`openings`), which the
  model frames. Rooms get their paint from `t_room` / `ROOM_NAMES` (0.36.19 layout: stair core NE, corridor z 90..93
  across the back, a hall x 51..54 on the main deck and B deck, doors at x 39/48/58; see `tower()`'s docstring).
  After changing the tower's sign names, delete `art_source/textures/bridge_tower_decals.png` so it repaints (0.36.21).
- Below decks (0.36.20): `below_decks` (origin 48, 0, 0, like the hull) is the hold deck, the engine room deck, the bow
  stairwell and the moon pool's rails on those decks, drawn by `make_riptide.py below_decks`
  (`art_source/scripts/make_riptide_below.py`; its signs are repainted on every build). Furniture is `BELOW_PROPS` in
  the generator; the engines, ballast tanks and the power cabinet are the gray box's own boxes, drawn by their size.
  The keel plate and the moon pool's trunk under the engine room deck are Site Zero's.
- Site Zero (0.36.30): `site_zero` (origin 48, 0, 0) is the lab's two decks, the moon pool's trunk below the engine
  room deck and the keel plate, drawn by `make_riptide.py site_zero` (`art_source/scripts/make_riptide_lab.py`; signs
  repainted on every build). Rooms come from `place()` in that script (keep it in step with `lab()`): it picks every
  wall stretch's, floor's and ceiling's paint and the sign over each door. Furniture is the gray box's own boxes with a
  `kind` plus `LAB_PROPS`. The glass (cleanroom, cells, director, security window, the hall's windows) is breakable
  panes from `glaze()` in `lab()`.
- The Leviathan's outline (0.36.17): `BOW_LINE` / `STERN_LINE` in `gen_riptide.py` (the bow narrows from z -93.5 to a
  2.8 m stem, the stern's corners round off at r 5). The plate there is yawed boxes (kind `hull_curve`), the decks
  `hull_slab` strips (kind `deck_curve`); `make_riptide.py` draws both smooth along the line. The sea's surface in the
  corners is `SeaFill` (`scripts/sea_fill.gd`, polygons), the water you swim in there is extra `SwimC*` strips.
