Bloxov: No Man's Land

A free blocky extraction shooter you play in your browser. Drop into the war-torn border town of Bloxov, loot what you can and get out alive.

  • Free to play, nothing to download
  • Raid solo, or online with friends and against other players
  • Fight scavs, raiders and the boss, Bon
  • Guns, attachments, traders and quests
  • Bring your loot home to your hideout and stash
