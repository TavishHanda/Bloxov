WHAT BLOXOV KEEPS ABOUT YOU

Bloxov: No Man's Land, by Tavish. Last changed 6 November 2026.

PvE (solo)

Your progress (stash, loadout, traders, record, character) and your settings stay on your device. There is no account. OPTIONS > SAVE TO FILE gives you a copy as a file to keep; it goes only to you.

Online play

The game server is sent the name you type and your connection's IP address, which it needs to put you in a raid. Other players in your raid see that name and your character. Online raids can also include AI-controlled PMCs, which collect nothing. The server's log keeps names, IP addresses and anti-cheat catches for a short time, to keep play fair.

PvP account (only if you make one)

Your username, your password scrambled (hashed) so it can't be read back, your PvP stash, money, loadout, stats, traders and quests, your raid results, and when you last played. No email and no real name are asked for. It's kept in a database hosted by Heroku until you delete it. Accounts not used for 12 months are deleted. If the account is banned for cheating, the ban's dates and reason are kept with it.

Getting a copy: in the game, ONLINE > PvP ACCOUNT > DOWNLOAD MY PvP DATA gives you everything the database keeps about your account as a file (never your password).

The website

On bloxov.com only, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits (which page, browser, country, how fast it loaded). It uses no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you. The phone and computer versions don't have it.

What Bloxov doesn't do

No ads, no tracking across other sites or apps, and nothing is sold or shared, apart from the hosts that run the game: Heroku (the server and database) and Cloudflare (the website).

Deleting your data

PvP account: in the game, ONLINE > PvP ACCOUNT > DELETE PvP ACCOUNT. Your account, PvP stash and everything with it are deleted after 30 days; signing in before then cancels it. The host's backups may keep a copy for a few days more. Can't get into the game? Email contact@bloxov.com with your username and we'll delete it (we may ask for your recovery code to check it's yours).

PvE progress: clear this site's data in your browser, or uninstall the game.
